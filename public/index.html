The Three Principles

Three universal constants behind all human experience, first articulated by Sydney Banks.

✨

Mind

The universal intelligence behind all of life — the formless energy and source from which all things are created.

💠

Consciousness

The gift of awareness that makes our thinking real to us — transforming formless thought into a felt, lived experience.

💭

Thought

The creative power that generates our moment-to-moment experience of reality — from the inside out.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Three Principles?
The Three Principles — Mind, Consciousness, and Thought — were first articulated by Sydney Banks. They point to the fundamental nature of how all human experience is created from the inside out. This is not a technique or method, but an understanding that naturally brings clarity and peace of mind.
How is this different from other coaching or self-help approaches?
Rather than offering techniques or strategies to manage your thinking, this understanding points you toward the source of your experience. When you see how thought creates your reality moment to moment, change happens naturally — without effort or practice.
Do I need to be a practitioner to join?
No. The community welcomes everyone — whether you're just curious about the Three Principles, exploring for personal growth, or a seasoned practitioner. The membership gives you access to publish events and resources, join discussions, and connect with others.
Can I browse the site without joining?
Yes. The Event Map, Resources Library, and Three Principles page are all freely accessible. The membership unlocks the ability to publish events, share resources, and participate in the community discussion feed and member directory.
